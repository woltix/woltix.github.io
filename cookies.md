A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. This page lists
every cookie woltix.com can set and what each one does.

<div class="wx-prose__note" markdown="1">
**Nothing optional runs before you choose.** Analytics stays switched off until
you click "Accept analytics". Rejecting is one click and takes effect
immediately — it is not harder than accepting.
</div>

We use these only to see aggregate figures — visits, pages, referrers,
country, device type. We have **not** enabled Google Signals, advertising
features, or remarketing on this property, and we do not combine analytics
data with anything you send us through the form.

## What we do not use

For the avoidance of doubt, woltix.com does **not** use:

- advertising or retargeting cookies;
- social media tracking pixels (Meta, LinkedIn Insight, X);
- session recording or heatmap tools;
- cross-site tracking of any kind;
- fingerprinting to work around your choice.

Fonts are served from our own domain rather than a font CDN, so no third party
receives your IP address simply because you loaded the page.

## Changing your mind

- **On this site:** click **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page. The
  banner reappears and your new choice applies immediately. If you switch
  analytics off, the `_ga` cookies already set are deleted straight away.
- **In your browser:** every major browser can block or delete cookies —
  see the help pages for
  [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647),
  [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac),
  [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
  or [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge).
- **Opt out of Google Analytics everywhere:** install Google's
  [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Blocking strictly necessary storage means the site cannot remember your cookie
choice, so the banner will appear on every visit.
