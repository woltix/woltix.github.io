## What we collect, and why

### 1. Information you send us {#information-you-send-us}

If you submit the demo request form we receive the **name** (optional),
**work email address** and **message** you type in.

- **Why:** to reply to your enquiry and, if it goes further, to arrange a demo.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, and our legitimate interest in responding to
  business enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(f)).
- **How long:** up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted. If you
  become a customer, your contact details move under the customer agreement.
- **We do not** add you to a newsletter, sell your details, or share them with
  anyone for their own marketing.

### 2. Analytics {#analytics}

With your permission we use **Google Analytics 4** to count visits and see
which pages are useful.

- **Why:** to understand how the site is used and improve it.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Analytics cookies are set
  **only after you click "Accept analytics"** in the cookie banner.
- **If you do not consent:** no analytics cookie or identifier is stored.
  Google receives a cookieless, non-identifying signal used only for
  aggregate estimates.
- IP addresses are shortened by Google before storage and are never used by us
  to identify individual visitors.

Details of every cookie are in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies/).

### 3. Server logs {#server-logs}

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub records standard request data
— IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, timestamp — for security and
to serve the site.

- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in operating and securing the site
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- We do not have access to these logs and cannot link them to you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** it if it is wrong;
- **delete** it ("right to be forgotten");
- **restrict** or **object to** our processing of it;
- **receive a portable copy** of it;
- **withdraw consent** at any time — this does not affect processing that
  already happened while consent was in place.

To exercise any of these, email
[{{ site.company.email }}](mailto:{{ site.company.email }}). We will respond
within 30 days. You never need an account, and it costs nothing.

You can change your cookie choice at any time using the **Cookie settings**
link in the footer of every page.

If you are in the EEA or UK and you think we have handled your data badly, you
may complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in the
UAE, you may contact the UAE Data Office.

## Children

This site is aimed at financial professionals. We do not knowingly collect
personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us
personal data, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

Traffic to woltix.com is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). Form submissions are sent
over an encrypted connection and delivered to a restricted internal mailbox.
No website is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and
organisational measures to protect the limited data this site collects.
